# ✨ Liquid Clip Studio

An ultra-modern, clean web application featuring **liquid glass aesthetics**, cool ambient animations, intelligent AI viral highlight detection, and a **CapCut-inspired Multi-Clip Timeline Editor** with full-resolution video exports.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Liquid Glass Design System**:
   - Ambient morphing liquid blobs, frosted glass cards with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.
   - Subtle glowing borders, tactile hover interactions, and smooth framer-motion-style CSS transitions.

2. **Universal Video Ingestion**:
   - **YouTube URL Fetcher**: Paste any YouTube video link (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`) to immediately extract metadata, chapters, and full-resolution streams.
   - **Local Video Uploader**: Drag & drop or browse any video file (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.webm`, `.mkv`) directly from your machine.

3. **AI Smart Clipper (Normal Page)**:
   - Automated viral highlight detection with Virality Badges (**🔥 98% Viral**, **⚡ 96% Peak Climax**, **💡 92% Key Insight**).
   - Dynamic animated captions preview with CapCut-style karaoke word highlighting.
   - Real-time aspect ratio converter (**9:16 Shorts/TikTok/Reels** with blurred ambient background, **16:9 Landscape**, **1:1 Square**).
   - One-click instant clip & download at full resolution.

4. **CapCut Studio (Multi-Clip Timeline Editor)**:
   - Real-time video player with live timecode readout (`Current`, `In`, `Out`, `Selection Duration`).
   - Interactive timeline scrubber with draggable playhead needle.
   - **Mark In (`[` / `I`)** and **Mark Out (`]` / `O`)** keyboard shortcuts and buttons.
   - **Multi-Area Selector**: Add multiple distinct video ranges as clips (`+ Add Area as Clip`).
   - Visual glowing multi-region blocks displayed on the timeline track in distinct colors.
   - **Clip Sequence Bin**: Preview individual clips in a loop, fine-tune ranges, or solo-export.
   - **Master Concat Reel**: 1-click **Export Merged Highlight Reel** merges all selected areas into one seamless high-resolution video.

5. **Full Resolution Video & Audio Downloader**:
   - Direct download of complete 1080p/4K MP4 videos and extracted 192kbps MP3 audio tracks.
